# Manufacturing Terms Glossary

## Excess Give Away (EGA)
Excess Give Away (EGA) or Extra Give Away refers to the extra amount of product packed into a package beyond the declared/theoretical target pack weight. In potato chip manufacturing, for instance, pack sizes are target-filled (e.g., 50g), but due to volumetric feeder or multihead weigher variances, actual weight might be slightly higher. Excess weight is "given away" to the consumer for free and represents direct raw material wastage.
Formula:
EGA % = ((Actual weight - Theoretical weight) / Actual weight) * 100

## Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)
OEE is a metric that evaluates how effectively a manufacturing operation is utilized. It is calculated by multiplying three factors:
1. **Availability**: Measures stopped time/downtime.
2. **Performance**: Measures speed losses (actual speed vs. target speed).
3. **Quality**: Measures quality losses (good products vs. rejects/failed bags).

## Loop Configuration
Loop ID or Loop configurations refer to the automated scheduling queues and feeding sequences on the raw material feeder lines. Loop configurations coordinate raw material flow to prevent multihead weigher starvation.
