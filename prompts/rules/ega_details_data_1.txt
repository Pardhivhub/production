TABLE: ega_details_data

1. Purpose:
Stores machine-wise hourly production and EGA (Extra Giveaway Amount) metrics. Tracks packaging weights, actual vs theoretical pack weights, and target limits to measure material giveaway loss.



2. Columns:
- t_weight: Total actual packed weight in grams.
- theoretical_pack_weight: Total theoretical target weight in grams.
- mean_weight: Arithmetic average weight of checked packs.
- machine_id: Foreign key to machines table.
- loop_id: ID of the production line (matches loop_id in gmiiot_lines).

3. Join Mappings:
- To join with machines: "ega_details_data JOIN machines ON ega_details_data.machine_id = machines.id".
- To join with lines: "ega_details_data JOIN gmiiot_lines ON ega_details_data.loop_id = gmiiot_lines.id".
