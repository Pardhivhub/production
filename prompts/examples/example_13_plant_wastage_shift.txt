Example 13 — Total wastage per plant and shift
Q: "Show total wastage in kg per plant name for each shift in June 2026"
SQL: SELECT p.plant_name, w.shift, SUM(w.wastage_kg) AS total_wastage_kg FROM wastage_records w JOIN gmiiot_plants p ON w.plant_id = p.plant_id WHERE w.production_start_time >= '2026-06-01' AND w.production_start_time < '2026-07-01' GROUP BY p.plant_name, w.shift ORDER BY total_wastage_kg DESC
